The route

Nine stops, one story

Select a stop
    • Route first. One 4px line, numbered stop discs, labels alternating above and below. Purple marks only the selected stop; the route ahead is dashed.

    The 2027 Vision

    Analog first.
    AI second.

    Founders are handing the thinking to AI. In 2027, Innovation Labs makes them do their own thinking first, then turns AI on that work as a critic.

    Why now
    +567%

    phone-free events worldwide across 2024 and 2025.

    Up to 55%

    less brain connectivity in ChatGPT users than in people writing unassisted.

    83%

    of ChatGPT users could not quote their own essay back.

    The learning loop
    1
    Think in analog

    Build version one yourself, on paper.

    2
    Think together

    Discuss it with peers.

    3
    Challenge with AI

    AI argues against your version.

    4
    Human synthesis

    The founder decides. Then repeat.

    What changes in 2027
    Mentors and coaches

    Two to three hands-on mentors per team, one business and one tech.

    A smaller cohort

    About 25 teams after Bootcamp 2 plus a week.

    The curriculum

    An Orientation, an AI literacy track, a buffer week.

    In short
    Build it on paper. Discuss it. Let AI argue with it. Repeat.

    Asked for one page? It fits on one page.

    When a request says one page, the output must not spill onto a second. The skill prioritizes, compresses and cuts lower-priority detail, then points to the Hub for the rest. It never solves overflow by shrinking text into unreadable density.

    1thesis per page
    3–5supporting modules
    15 mmmargins, 12-column grid
    • Print-safe cards. Filled with surface-tint instead of borders, no glows, no transparency over photos.
    • Real links only. The call to action uses the current Hub URL you supply, never an old artifact link found in the source.
    Tone lenses

    One message. Three voices.

    A lens changes the voice. The facts, the structure and the look stay exactly where they were.

    Source factabout 25 teamsafter Bootcamp 253 hours per team in 2026
    Formalformal-lens
    In 2027, Innovation Labs will focus on about 25 teams after Bootcamp 2, so each receives more than the 53 hours of mentoring delivered per team in 2026.

    Precise and concise. Confident without hype, no corporate filler.

    Friendlyfriendly-lens
    From 2027 we're keeping things smaller: about 25 teams after Bootcamp 2, so every team gets more than the 53 hours of mentoring they had in 2026.

    Warm and direct. Contractions welcome, never childish.

    Jokeyjokey-lens
    Fewer teams, more mentor per team. About 25 teams continue after Bootcamp 2, and each gets more than the 53 hours of 2026. The math is on your side.

    The rule of one

    For a normal task, use

    • one output skill
    • zero or one tone lens
    • one design system

    Stack several output skills only when you ask for a multi-format campaign.

    How to use

    Attach your source. Say what you need.

    Name the format and, if it matters, the audience. The plugin picks the skill and only says what your source says.

    Built in

    Portable design rules

    The plugin carries its own Innovation Labs design system and tokens, so it works even when the native Innovation Labs Design System is not installed.

    Optional

    Native Claude Design System

    Select it alongside the plugin for a richer editor and runtime of the same rules. It mirrors the bundled spec and never overrides it.

    Optional

    Humanizer, last

    The public Humanizer skill can polish narrative copy once structure is approved. Not a dependency, and not for tables, labels, citations or numbers.
