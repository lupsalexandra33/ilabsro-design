MVP scope

Covered

  • Reading IL strategy docs, event scripts and program notes
  • Candidate themes and angles, traceable to their source
  • Evaluation gate: fidelity and distinctness, plus four quality scores
  • Only evaluated themes tracked in Notion
  • Human selection: Selected, Later or Rejected
  • Source-grounded brief handed to the existing generation workflow
  • Human review of the resulting draft

Left for later

  • Publishing and scheduling
  • Social platform integrations and automation tooling
  • Feedback loops from performance data
  • Production hardening (permissions, monitoring, scale)
