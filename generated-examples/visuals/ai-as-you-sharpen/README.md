## What this is

An Innovation Labs explanatory visual showing a human-first AI workflow:

the person brings the first answer, AI challenges it with questions, and the person sharpens the result.

## What it tests

- autonomous visual-direction selection;
- translation of an abstract principle into a simple sequence;
- clear human ownership of the thinking;
- AI used as a challenger rather than a replacement;
- concise embedded copy;

## Why we kept it

The visual explains the human/AI relationship clearly without relying on generic robot imagery or a text-heavy infographic.

It shows that `brand-image-maker` can independently choose an appropriate explanatory structure for an abstract concept.
