# Innovation Labs Instagram Post Maker — experimental

Use for single social cards or short Instagram carousels.

## Rules

1. Use the native Innovation Labs Design System when available; otherwise read `../../design-system/INNOVATION_LABS_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
2. Use only approved/source-backed claims, figures, dates and quotes.
3. Supported defaults: 1080×1080 square, 1080×1350 portrait, 1080×1920 story, 1200×627 link card. Prefer 1080×1350 for feed posts unless requested otherwise.
4. For a carousel, default to 3–7 cards:
   - hook;
   - one idea per card;
   - clear final takeaway/CTA.
5. Keep one message per asset: kicker + 64–96px headline at 1080px wide or one 160–240px stat. Aim for ~12 words maximum on canvas.
6. Grounds are solid teal, deep teal, surface-tint or white; Night is allowed for countdown/quote cards. No mesh, confetti or emoji. One playful device maximum.
7. Logo sits bottom-left or bottom-right with 64px safe margin (story: 250px top/bottom). Photos must be real IL people/events and carry text on a solid color block.
8. A `friendly-lens` or restrained `jokey-lens` may be applied when appropriate.

Do not turn an entire strategy document into a 20-card carousel. Select one story.
