The 2027 Vision

Analog first.
AI second.

Founders are handing the thinking to AI. In 2027, Innovation Labs makes them do their own thinking first, then turns AI on that work as a critic.

What changes in 2027

Mentors and coaches

Two to three hands-on mentors per team, one business and one tech. Top mentors write one shared rubric, so teams stop hearing six contradictory opinions.

A smaller cohort

Intake halved, from 80 teams to 41, and Demo Day still had 17. In 2027 the program focuses on about 25 teams after Bootcamp 2 plus a week, so each team gets more than today's 53 hours of mentoring.

The curriculum

An Orientation states the AI position once. Bootcamp 1 gets an AI literacy track. Paper exercises, like a competitor war wall, fill existing sessions. A buffer week follows Bootcamp 2.

In the room

Competitor war wall

Printed competitor one-pagers on a wall. Teams sort them into direct, indirect and ecosystem.

Journey maps on butcher paper

A long paper roll on a wall. Teams draw the journey by hand and walk through it standing up.

Elevator pitch swap

Write your one-line pitch on a card. A stranger team pitches it back using only the card.

What AI is, and isn't
AI is

a challenger, a critic, a devil's advocate, a reviewer, a stress tester, a debate partner, a perspective generator.

AI is not

the founder, the product manager, the storyteller, the strategist.

In short

Build it on paper. Discuss it. Let AI argue with it. Repeat.
