# Friendly lens

This is a voice modifier, not an output maker.

- Keep facts, numbers, claims and structure unchanged.
- Use natural, direct sentences.
- Prefer warmth and clarity over polished corporate language.
- Small contractions and conversational phrasing are welcome.
- Do not become childish or overly casual.
