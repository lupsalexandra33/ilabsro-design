# Website patterns

## Landing hero

Preferred structure:
- left: thesis, short context, one or two actions;
- right: meaningful visual, such as a compact route/map preview;
- do not place the entire strategy in the hero.

## Deep dives

- Continuous editorial flow with subtle section changes.
- Light mode: alternate white and baby teal.
- Night mode: alternate deep ink/deep teal.
- Use modular content: short intro, takeaways, evidence, stats, diagrams, expandable detail.

## Map behavior

Good:
- numbered stops;
- one clear path;
- click/tap stop for details;
- route highlight on selected stop;
- a quiet backdrop.

Avoid:
- dense animated mesh;
- dozens of tiny reactive lines;
- background motion that competes with the route.


## Section selection

- Build sections that have a clear audience job and source-backed content.
- Do not add a generic `Open Threads` / `Open Questions` ending by default.
- Keep unresolved evidence, estimates or limitations local to the section they qualify.

## Strategy route fidelity

For the main strategy map, follow `map-proposal-pattern.md`. The desktop route should feel like one connected visual journey, not a generic timeline or card grid.
