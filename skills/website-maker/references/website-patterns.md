# Website patterns — v0.4

## Landing hero

Preferred structure:
- left: thesis, short context, one or two actions;
- right: meaningful visual such as a compact route/map preview;
- do not place the entire strategy in the hero.

A brand gradient or approved decorative element may be used in the hero when `brand-elements` is active. Reading sections remain flat.

## Deep dives

- Continuous editorial flow with subtle section changes.
- Light: alternate white/pale teal.
- Night: alternate deep ink/deep teal.
- Use modular content: short intro, takeaways, evidence, stats, diagrams, expandable detail.

## Fluid layout

- Header, rail and body align to one shared container.
- Use continuous clamp/fluid sizing rather than patchy breakpoint jumps.
- Long labels wrap.
- Portrait adapts; do not shrink the desktop composition.
- Check laptop, phone landscape and phone portrait.

## Map behavior

Good:
- numbered stops;
- one clear path;
- tap/click stop for detail;
- route highlight on selected stop;
- quiet backdrop;
- full + mini map generated from one shared stop model within a Hub.

Avoid:
- decorative/template mesh behind the map;
- dense animated networks;
- background motion competing with the route.

## Section selection

- Build sections with a clear audience job and source-backed content.
- Do not add a generic `Open Threads` / `Open Questions` ending by default.
- Keep unresolved evidence, estimates or limitations local to the claim they qualify.
