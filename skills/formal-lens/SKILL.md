# Formal lens

This is a voice modifier, not an output maker.

- Keep facts, numbers, claims and structure unchanged.
- Prefer precise, concise language.
- Sound confident without hype.
- Reduce slang and jokes.
- Avoid bureaucratic/corporate filler.
- Preserve the Innovation Labs human voice.
